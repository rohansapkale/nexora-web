MediTrack Web

Enterprise HQ Workspace, Reconciliations & Auditing

The centralized administrative command center for pharmaceutical operations. Coordinates complex relationships between doctors, chemists, stockists, and headquarters teams with sub-second data loading and auditable workflows.

Primary Technology Stack
Angular 20 TypeScript NGXS Store RxJS Reactive Bootstrap 5 Angular Material NSwag Typed Proxies SCSS Modules
  • Compilation Engine: Multi-tiered reconciliation engine that resolves dispatches, chemist claims, and doctor orders into verifiable ledgers.
  • Hierarchical Requisitions: Automated state machine managing monetary budgets and multi-stage branch sign-offs.
  • AI Import Hub: Intelligent document extraction and validation pipeline for stockist inventory files.
  • Enterprise RBAC: JWT Bearer authentication with granular permission guards preventing field-role access to sensitive HQ registries.
Mobile Native Ionic 8 • Capacitor 7

MediTrack Mob

Medical Representative (MR) Operations, Reporting & Schemes

A high-velocity mobile companion built for Medical Representatives in the field. Engineered for high responsiveness, offline calculation resilience, tactile feedback, and instantaneous territory customer lookup.

Primary Technology Stack
Ionic 8 Capacitor 7.2 Angular 19/20 ApexCharts Ng-Rive Micro-motion Android Native Capacitor Preferences Offline Caching
  • Field Customer Profiling: Instant directory of assigned Doctors, Chemists, and Stockists with quick call and navigation triggers.
  • Interactive Sales Telemetry: High-DPI ApexCharts rendering monthly trends, investment ratios, and customer allocations on mobile screens.
  • Pharma Scheme Calculator: 100% offline mathematical engine calculating PTS, PTR, bonus ratios, and chemist ROI during sales calls.
  • Capacitor Native Bridge: Hardware-accelerated transitions, native status bar handling, and secure keystore token storage.
